# Decision record

## Decision
One sentence stating the choice.

## Context
What constraint or problem made a decision necessary?

## Options considered
| Option | Benefit | Cost / risk |
|---|---|---|

## Chosen option
What was selected and why?

## Consequences
What becomes easier? What becomes harder? What new obligations are introduced?

## Evidence and assumptions
Separate facts that were checked from assumptions that still need validation.

## Review trigger
What future event or evidence should cause this decision to be revisited?
