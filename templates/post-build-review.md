## Acceptance criteria
| Criterion | Evidence | Result |
|---|---|---|

## What went well
Keep this specific and evidence-based.

## What did not go as expected
Include failed approaches and useful negative results.

## Risks and limitations
What is still fragile, unsupported, or unverified?

## Next improvements
Prioritise by user impact and risk, not by how interesting the technology is.

## Reusable lesson
